This is the camera my family used in the '80s and '90s before going digital in the early '00s. I only really used it for a few rolls, as it has several technical issues, and even in pristine condition it has several drawbacks compared to my Minolta so it wouldn't make much sense for me to use it. With all that said, it's still a lovely camera, with all the quirks and oddities of soviet camera design, and it definitely holds sentimental value to me.
